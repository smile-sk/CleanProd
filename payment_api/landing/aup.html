CleanTrade Payment API

Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

CleanTrade is a USDT (TRC20) payment confirmation API. Every order carries the merchant's own TRC20 wallet: the buyer pays to that address and we match the order amount on-chain, then notify your server. CleanTrade never holds the funds and never opens or assigns wallets to merchants. Still, we are responsible for what our service is used to facilitate, and we reserve the right to suspend or terminate any account that violates this policy.

What you may NOT do

You may not use CleanTrade to accept payments for, or in connection with:

  • illegal goods or services in your jurisdiction or ours;
  • fraud, scams, phishing, ponzi or pyramid schemes, or misleading investment offers;
  • malware, exploits, stolen data, or hacking services;
  • child sexual abuse material, or any content that exploits minors;
  • sanctions evasion, or payments involving sanctioned persons, entities, or jurisdictions;
  • weapons, narcotics, or other controlled items sold without a licence;
  • transactions that violate applicable financial, AML, or export regulations.

Merchant responsibility

You are solely responsible for what you sell, for your own KYC/AML obligations where they apply to you, and for your buyers' privacy and consent. CleanTrade never holds funds, so refunds and disputes are settled directly between you and your buyer on-chain.

How a payment is matched, and the evidence we keep

An order is credited when ALL of the following are true:

  • an incoming USDT (TRC20) transfer reaches the wallet named in that order;
  • its amount equals the order amount (tolerance 0.005 USDT, two decimals);
  • its block time is later than the moment the order was created;
  • its transaction id has not already been credited to another order.

If any of these is missing, the order is not credited and no webhook is sent. A transfer that arrives after the order has expired is not credited either: the money is already on the merchant's wallet and settling it is a matter between the merchant and the buyer. Orders are matched by amount and wallet only - not by the buyer's name, memo, or a screenshot.

The TRON blockchain is the authoritative record of payments, and it is public: anyone can check the wallet and the transfer on Tronscan without our involvement. On top of that, for every order we keep an append-only audit log - when the order was created, which wallet and amount were expected, how many times our detector queried the blockchain and what it saw, and what our webhook delivery received in response. The merchant may request that record for a specific order; it is the document we rely on when a payment is disputed.

We are responsible for performing the check described above honestly and for keeping its record; we are not a party to the sale between the merchant and the buyer, we do not hold or move the funds, and we do not guarantee the buyer's behaviour, the delivery of goods or the merchant's own obligations. To the extent permitted by law, our total liability for any claim relating to the service is limited to the subscription fees actually paid for the three months preceding the claim, and we are not liable for lost profit, lost revenue or indirect damages. Nothing in this policy limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

Enforcement

We may suspend or terminate API access at any time for conduct that violates this policy, harms CleanTrade's infrastructure or reputation, or risks our hosting providers and partners. Where required, we will cooperate with law enforcement.
