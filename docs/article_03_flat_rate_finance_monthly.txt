 Why flat-rate pricing beats percentage fees on crypto payment gateways

Most crypto gateways take a percentage of every payment. The leaders - NOWPayments and Plisio - charge 0.5% per transaction; Coinbase Commerce, CoinGate and BitPay sit at 1% or more. It sounds small. Let's do the math that actually matters.

 The honest math

Say a gateway takes 0.5% per payment. Here's what that costs at different monthly volumes, next to a flat $29.99 subscription (CleanTrade Pro):

Monthly volume 0.5% gateway fee Flat $29.99 Difference
--- --- --- ---
$1,000 $5 $29.99 percentage wins by $24.99
$3,000 $15 $29.99 percentage wins by $14.99
$6,000 $30 $29.99 ~break-even
$10,000 $50 $29.99 flat wins by $20.01
$50,000 $250 $29.99 flat wins by $220.01

Be honest about the break-even: a 0.5% gateway is cheaper below roughly $6,000/month (for the $9.99 Starter tier, the break-even is ~$2,000/month). Flat pricing wins as you scale past that, and the gap only widens from there.

 Why percentage fees feel harmless but aren't

1. They scale against you. Percentage fees grow with your success. A flat fee stays flat. Your cost structure should be predictable, not a bet against your own growth.
2. They stack with everything else. Network fees, FX spread on crypto-to-fiat, withdrawal fees - the "0.5%" is rarely the only cost.
3. They punish high-ticket items. Sell a $499 course at 1%? That's $5 per sale. At volume, it adds up.

Flat pricing means: you know your costs, forever. $29.99/month, 0% per payment, no surprise deductions.

 The real reason to care isn't the fee - it's custody and DX

Flat vs percentage is a real question, but it's the second question. The first is: where does the money live?

- Custodial gateways hold your funds on their balance until you withdraw - and can freeze or delay payouts.
- Non-custodial means the payment lands directly on your own TRC20 wallet from the first second.

CleanTrade is non-custodial and flat: $29.99/month (Pro), 0% per payment, money straight to your wallet, no KYC, API key in about a minute, webhooks with retries.

 The one case where percentage wins

If you process under ~$6,000/month, a 0.5% gateway is cheaper than $29.99 flat. That's exactly why a good flat-rate provider also offers a free tier - so small merchants can start free and grow into flat pricing on the same integration, without switching gateways twice.

 Bottom line

Flat pricing isn't a magic 10x saving - at realistic competitor rates it wins by a margin, not an order of magnitude. The bet worth making is on self-custody and DX: a gateway that never touches your money and takes three lines of code to integrate.
